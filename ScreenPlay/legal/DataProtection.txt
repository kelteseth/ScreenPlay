DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY NOTICE
=====================================

ScreenPlay is committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

CRASH REPORTING
---------------
ScreenPlay uses Sentry's open source tier to collect crash reports for the sole purpose of improving the ScreenPlay open source application. This helps us identify and fix bugs to provide you with a better user experience.

What we collect:
- Technical crash information (error messages, stack traces)
- System information (OS version, hardware specifications)
- Application state at the time of the crash

What we DO NOT collect:
- Personal information (names, email addresses, contact details)
- User-created content (wallpapers, widgets, personal files)
- Any data that could identify you personally

DATA PROCESSING
---------------
All crash data is processed by Sentry under their open source tier terms. The data is used exclusively for debugging and improving ScreenPlay's stability and performance. We do not share, sell, or use this information for any other purpose.

YOUR RIGHTS
-----------
Since we do not collect personal information, standard data protection rights (access, rectification, erasure) do not apply to the crash data we collect. However, you can disable crash reporting at any time through the application settings. You must restart ScreenPlay or any Wallpaper or Widget for it to take effect.
